Hello world.

This is the first post on deveshgautam.com. I am starting this blog to write in public — to capture what I am building, what I am learning, and what I am thinking about, one post at a time.

The plan is simple: publish something every day. Some days it will be a short note. Other days it will be a longer write-up about a project, a tool I tried, or a problem I solved. The goal is not to go viral. The goal is to show up consistently and leave a trail I can look back on.

This site itself is the first project I am documenting. It is a static Astro site, edited through Pages CMS, and hosted on Cloudflare. You can read about how it all fits together in the next post.

If you are reading this, thank you. There is more coming tomorrow.
